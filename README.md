# RW2019DHS
Code for malaria-focused analysis of 2019-20 Rwanda DHS

Objective: Provide nationally-representative measures of malaria infection in Rwandan adults sampled in the 2019-20 Rwanda Demographics and Health Survey (RDHS).

Methods: Perform malaria-specific quantitative PCR (qPCR) on dried blood spot (DBS) samples collected for DHS HIV evaluation. 50% of DBS samples were randomly selected from each capture area (cluster), then samples determined malaria-positive by RDT or microscopy performed in the DHS and those within clusters reporting high (>15%) malaria prevalence by RDT or microscopy were added to the sample set.

The folowing steps were taken
1. Randomly select 50% of DBS from each of 500 clusters.
2. Identify clusters with high (>/=15%) malaria prevalence using RDHS-reported RDT or microscopy-based Plasmodium spp. detection data. Samples that were not initially selected were added to the final data set.
3. Determine qPCR positivity of samples
